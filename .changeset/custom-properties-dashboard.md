New: the dashboard shows the custom properties a [hook script](https://scoutui.dev/docs/guides/add-custom-properties) sets on your components. A repo's **Components** tab offers a **Properties** facet to filter by one or more property values, and **Columns** to add up to 3 properties as sortable columns; a component's page lists them all under **Properties**. The chart builder can chart one property value as its own series with **Add a property series**.
